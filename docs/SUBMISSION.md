# Submission checklist and evidence

## Positioning

**SignalLoop is the content studio that can show what it learned.**

For a podcast creator who repeatedly rewrites AI captions, SignalLoop records the edit, proposes a scoped preference, asks for approval, and shows its effect on untouched moments. Audience feedback is a separate, bounded signal. Source guard flags a cut or caption that may change the speaker's meaning. The campaign planner connects those decisions to a usable review-and-export workflow.

Do not pitch this as a unique invention of brand memory, a trained virality predictor, or demonstrated audience growth. The stronger claim is observable, reviewable behavior.

## Rehearsal: the claim hierarchy

1. **Demonstrated:** saved edits, approved scoped rules, controlled draft comparison, measured rank changes from imported observations, guarded scheduling, actual encoded files.
2. **Implementation tested with provider fixtures:** structured Gemini review rejects invented clip IDs and non-source quotes; this is not evidence of a successful live provider call.
3. **Needs your verification:** live AI credentials, local Whisper, real podcast input, hosted deployment and final three-minute recording.
4. **Not built:** social OAuth/publishing, live analytics sync, face tracking, multi-user tenancy.

Memory proof reports deterministic rule adherence, not creative quality, causal uplift or model generalization. Source guard is a conservative lexical screen, not a full factual verifier. Caption phrase times are interpolated within original source segments, not forced-aligned word times.

## Distinctive live moment

Remove a hashtag in one clip; approve that preference. Show the same rule removing the hashtag in a different clip without changing YouTube's copy. Then paste a caption with an invented numerical claim into Source guard and show the block. Finally, show actual audience observations (or clearly labeled test observations) changing clip rankings without overriding the creator's language rules.

That demonstrates **voice, source meaning and audience evidence as separate controllable inputs**, not one opaque generated score.
